## 1. FAQ Digest

|---|------------------------|--------------|-------------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | What are personality subselves like? | Discrete brain regions; each has unique talents, a primary job, own thoughts/feelings; benign intent (each means well from its view); can be active/quiet; equal value; live in present or past; communicate via inner voices, images, feelings, sensations; have genders/ages distinct from body. | "Parts are metaphors, not real." → They map to discrete neural networks; not killable/exilable. | Learn article: "Meet Your Inner Team" |
| 2 | If I have parts, who is "me"? | "Me/I/self" (lowercase) = whole person. "Self" (capital S) = natural leader part. When blended, "I" refers to the controlling part, not the whole. | "I am my thoughts/feelings." → You are the observer; blending creates confusion. | Onboarding tooltip: "Who's speaking right now?" |
| 3 | What is the true Self like? | Natural leader: perceives reality, seeks counsel, delegates, coordinates, decides calmly, acts, responds. Like a skilled conductor/coach. Feels full emotions. | 4 | How do I know Self is in charge? | Feelings: calm, centered, grounded, clear, compassionate, decisive, purposeful — even in crisis. Behavioral signs: wise decisions, emotional range, flexibility. | "Self-leadership feels numb/flat." → It feels alive, engaged, emotionally present. | FAQ page + self-check assessment |
| 5 | Where do subselves come from? | Form automatically in response to trauma/neglect (often in childhood). Protective parts never forget; guard long after threat gone. Stuck parts hold outdated beliefs. | "Parts are birth defects or pathology." → They're adaptive survival responses. | Learn article: "Why Parts Form" |
| 6 | What do subselves want? | Safety (as they define it); keep their roles; be recognized, trusted, respected, appreciated. Fear being fired/killed/banished. | "Difficult parts want to hurt me." → All parts protect; their methods may be outdated. | Journaling prompt: "What is this part trying to protect?" |
| 7 | Do my parts interact with others' parts? | Constantly. Disowned parts attract partners with similar active parts. Relationships = 3-layer dynamics: my inner conflicts + your inner conflicts + cross-part clashes. | "Relationship problems are about the other person." → Both inner systems drive the dance. | Learn article: "Parts in Relationships" |
| 8 | How do typical subselves behave? | Example: "Hulk" (Guardian) caused back pain/insomnia to force planning time; protected a Shamed Child. When befriended & retrained, symptom vanished. | "Physical symptoms are purely medical." → Parts can cause/relieve somatic symptoms. | Problem track starter: "Somatic Signals" |
| 9 | Is meeting inner family dangerous? | No. Guardians/Kids may resist initially; shift to cooperation when they trust intent is appreciation & help. No "demons unleashed" in 20+ years practice. | "Parts work = losing control / psychosis." → You control pace; it's fail-safe. | Onboarding tooltip: "You're in control" |
| 10 | How can I meet my subselves? | Inner dialog: visualize, interview, negotiate, retrain, harmonize. Start with Self-to-part dialog exercise. | "I need a therapist to do this." → Self-guided basics are accessible; therapist for depth. | Guided exercise: "First Inner Dialog" |

## 3. Misconceptions & Gotchas for Beginners

| Misconception | Reality | Beginner Trap |
|---------------|---------|---------------|
| "Parts are separate personalities / multiple personality disorder." | Normal multiplicity: subselves are functional modules; DID is extreme fragmentation. | Over-pathologizing normal inner diversity. |
| "Bad parts exist (Critic, Saboteur, Addict)." | All parts have benign intent; methods are outdated. | Fighting/banishing parts → increases resistance. |
| "Self is a spiritual higher self / pure consciousness." | Self is a subself with leadership talent; feels emotions; can't force parts. | Expecting Self to be emotionless/perfect; frustration when blending occurs. |
| "Blending = failure." | Blending is normal; noticing it IS Self-leadership emerging. | Shame spirals when blended; missing the data point. |
| "I need to visualize to do parts work." | Non-visual people get voices, feelings, sensations, knowing. | Giving up because "I can't see images." |
| "Once a part transforms, it's done." | Parts can revert under stress; trust builds over repetitions. | Declaring victory too early; not maintaining inner relationships. |
| "Resistance means the method doesn't work." | Resistance = Blocker/Saboteur doing its job; shift focus to THAT part. | Quitting instead of befriending the resistant part. |
| "Parts work replaces therapy/medication." | Complementary; organic factors may need medical support. | Stopping needed treatment; using parts work for crisis stabilization alone. |
| "My parts are exactly like the examples." | Each person's inner family is unique; examples are archetypes. | Forcing fit; missing idiosyncratic parts (e.g., "pile of black dirt"). |
| "Intellectual understanding = integration." | Integration requires repeated felt experience, not just insight. | Reading without practicing dialogs; "knowing" without "meeting." |

## 4. Terminology Digest

| Source Term | Plain-Language Consumer Equivalent | Notes |
|-------------|-----------------------------------|-------|
| Subself / Part | Inner part / aspect of you | Avoid "subself" in UI; use "part" or "aspect" |
| True Self (capital S) | Your Inner Leader / Core Self | Capitalize only in glossary; UI: "your Inner Leader" |
| False Self | Protective Autopilot / Survival Self | Not "fake self" — it's a protective coalition |
| Inner Child / Kids | Young Part / Inner Child | "Kids" plural okay; avoid "wounded child" as label |
| Guardian | Protector / Guardian Part | Subtypes: Numb-er, Critic, Perfectionist, Worrier, Catastrophizer, Saboteur, Blocker, Aggressor, Addict |
| Manager | Helper Part / Manager Part | Nurturer, Analyzer, Teacher, Organizer, etc. |
| Firefighter | Crisis Responder / Emergency Part | Reactive protectors (addiction, rage, dissociation) |
| Blending | Takeover / Merging / "I got hijacked" | UI: "blended with [part name]" |
| Unblending | Stepping back / Separating | "Notice the part; you're not it" |
| Exile | Stuck Young Part / Burdened Part | IFS term; avoid in consumer UI |
| Burden | Old Pain / Outdated Belief | What a part carries from the past |
| Reassign / Retrain | New Role / Updated Job | Positive framing; not "fixing" |
| Inner Family / Inner Team | Your Inner Team / Inner Crew | "Family" can imply bio-family baggage |
| Grown Wounded Child (GWC) | Adult carrying childhood wounds | Avoid label in UI; describe the pattern |
| Polarization | Inner Tug-of-War / Inner Conflict | Two+ parts locked in opposition |
| Alliance | Inner Partnership / Team-Up | Parts working together |
| Blocking | Interference / Static | Blocker part prevents access |

- **Inner Team Map** (interactive, not scored): User creates part cards; app suggests tracks based on patterns (e.g., many young parts → "Rescuing Young Parts" track; strong Critic/Perfectionist → "Worth Reclaimed" track).

- **Blending Spotter** (in-moment): "Who's driving right now?" quick tag → builds awareness dataset for personalized insights.

## 6. Do-Not-Use List

### Proprietary / Branded Terms (Exclude from App Copy)
- **SFHELP**, **SFhelp.org**, **Lesson 1/2/3/4/5/6/7** (site-specific curriculum)
- **Grown Wounded Child / GWC** (author's branded framework label)
- **"Who's Really Running Your Life?"** (book title)
- **Dr. Richard Schwartz / Internal Family Systems Therapy / IFS** (trademarked model name — use "parts work" or "inner-family work" generically)
- **Center for Self Leadership / selfleadership.org** (org name)
- **Hal & Sidra Stone / Voice Dialog** (specific modality)
- **Truddi Chase / "When Rabbit Howls"** (case study)
- **John Rowan / "Subpersonalities"** (author/book)
- **James Masterson / "Search for Our Real Self"** (author/book)
- **Robert Subby / "Healing the Family Within"** (author/book)
- **Jacqui Bishop & Mary Grunte** (authors)
- **Facebook group links, YouTube video embeds, specific URLs**
- **"20 years' experience," "24 years' clinical study," "hundreds of clients"** (personal credentials)
- **Specific client anecdotes** (e.g., "Hulk," "curly-haired five-year-old girl," "pile of black dirt," "pack of ferrets") — these are identifiable case material
- **Polls, poll links, "what people think" references**
- **Medical claims language**: "cure," "heal permanently," "end [condition]" — use "reduce," "manage," "support recovery from"

### Personal / Specific Details to Exclude
- Author's first-person voice ("I believe," "my clients," "in my practice")
- Specific timestamps ("2025-12-18 Rating" column)
- Site navigation artifacts ("pop/", "gwc/", "ifs/", relative links)
- HTML/iframe artifacts (YouTube embeds, colorbutton.gifs, tri-red.gifs)
- "See also" / cross-reference link lists to other site pages
- Worksheet/link references by filename (e.g., "links1.htm#worksheets")

### Framing to Avoid
- Pathologizing language: "disorder," "illness," "symptom," "patient," "treatment"
- Guru/founder-centric framing ("Schwartz says," "the model teaches")
- Prescriptive "should" language ("you should free your Self")
- Gendered defaults for parts (parts can be any/no gender)
- Ageist language about "childish" parts — use "young" or "developmentally young"
